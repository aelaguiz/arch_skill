We watched 103 PostHog recordings from this 48-hour window: 24 first visits by brand new people (19 devices), 25 returning users, 27 sessions that touched a paywall, a checkout or a purchase, and 27 random draws, on both platforms, almost all on build 2.1.41 with a few on 2.1.40 and one on 2.1.39. Most of the tape is people quietly finishing lessons and the daily puzzle. Below is what looked bad or odd, what is already known, and what we could not see.

How to read this page
 Every item says what it looks like, how many watched sessions showed it, what the warehouse says about how often it happens, and where to look. Whether something is a mistake is the reader's call. Every frame was opened and checked against its caption before publishing. Timestamps in the recording links are seconds from the start of the recording and can be off by about 10 seconds against the event clock. A fix merged to main is not on any phone watched here: every session ran a build cut before the merges named below.

Things we noticed about our own tracking

For the data owner. One line each. Nothing here is a headline.

No event marks a process death. session_summary.end_reason=process_start with explicit_close=false is on 1,414 of 1,522 summaries in the window (every session that is not a rollover), so it cannot serve as a restart marker; only the gap method (a cold start with no app_background before it) separates a death in the foreground from an ordinary background kill.
