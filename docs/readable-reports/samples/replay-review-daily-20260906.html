Replay review, living daily page

What users actually did: session replays from 2026-09-04 08:00 UTC to 2026-09-06 08:00 UTC

Run date 2026-09-06 Model anthropic/claude-fable-5-1 (orchestrator at thinking high, every child at thinking medium) Source: PostHog session recordings, project 215955, plus BigQuery react_production for frequencies

We watched 103 PostHog recordings from this 48-hour window: 24 first visits by brand new people (19 devices), 25 returning users, 27 sessions that touched a paywall, a checkout or a purchase, and 27 random draws, on both platforms, almost all on build 2.1.41 with a few on 2.1.40 and one on 2.1.39. Every sample was a seeded random draw from the full recording list for its population; the one followed thread is labeled and kept out of the counts. Most of the tape is people quietly finishing lessons and the daily puzzle. Below is what looked bad or odd, what is already known, and what we could not see.

How to read this page Every item says what it looks like, how many watched sessions showed it, what the warehouse says about how often it happens, and where to look. Whether something is a mistake is the reader's call. Every frame was opened and checked against its caption before publishing. Timestamps in the recording links are seconds from the start of the recording and can be off by about 10 seconds against the event clock. A fix merged to main is not on any phone watched here: every session ran a build cut before the merges named below.

This looks bad: on Android the app dies while the person is using it and comes back on the "Checking for Updates" boot screen, somewhere else

A person is in the middle of something; ten to twenty seconds later the app is cold-booting and they land on a different screen. Three cases hold up after the adversary re-timed each one against the event log:

  • On the sign-up screen, brand new install (OnePlus). The sign-up page is on screen; ten seconds later it is the boot screen. The next recording opens on "Ready to begin?" and the person does the same onboarding puzzle a second time, then taps Continue six times on a mid-onboarding question and leaves for good in the window. Starting onboarding again from the welcome screen after a signed-out restart is how the code works today; there is no path that resumes a signed-out person at the sign-up page.
  • Under the Google Play purchase sheet, brand new install (moto g play). The person tapped TRY FOR $0.00, the store sheet opened (never on camera), and 75 seconds later the next recording opens on the boot screen and "Setting up your account...". No purchase result of any kind was ever logged for that tap. The app never wrote a "went to background" event, which the Play sheet normally causes, so this reads as the process gone under the store sheet rather than a death in plain view.
  • On the Puzzles tab right after the chapter reward screens, returning user (Samsung). "You earned 20 gems!", Continue, the Puzzles tab for two seconds, then the boot screen, then the Learn tab, which is not where they were.

We saw it 3 times: on 2 of 19 new-user devices and 1 of 25 returning users, all Android 2.1.41. Two more restarts that the investigators first read the same way are not counted: in one (Play vs AI, hand 4 of 5) the app went to the background 2.5 seconds after the Fold tap and cold-started 20 seconds later, and in the other (a returning user under the Play sheet) the app had been in the background about six and a half minutes before the relaunch; both look like ordinary background kills or the person reopening the app, and they are listed under "What we could not see".

I checked the data: rare, and not clearly an Android thing. Counting cold starts whose device was in the foreground and active less than 60 seconds earlier, Android shows 32 of 1,024 cold starts (about 3 percent) and iOS 17 of 942 (about 2 percent) in the window (react_production.splash_seen against activity and lifecycle events). 13 of 339 sign-up screens in the window were followed by a cold start within two minutes (11 Android, 2 iOS), 7 of them with no sign-in attempt in between (react_production.onboarding_handoff_created against splash_seen, Sept 4 to 6). Sentry has no crash row for any of the three devices at those moments, and it does not record an operating-system memory kill, so the cause cannot be told from here. Play Console vitals for 2.1.41 build 702 would settle it (see "Needs a human").

Where to look: sign-up screen at 1:01, then the next recording at 0:05; moto g play, TRY tapped at 3:53, then the next boot at 0:00; Puzzles tab restart at 4:36 to 4:48.

No issue names a restart on the sign-up screen or after the reward screens. What we could not see

Capture limits met this run, items that could not be settled from the recordings, and what was cut.

Capture limits

  • Native store sheets (Google Play, App Store), the Google, Apple and phone sign-in sheets, iOS permission dialogs, and the welcome overlay are never on camera. Where they matter, the page shows the frame before and the frame after and says so.
  • Frames arrive about every 5 seconds, so anything shorter (the 54 ms paywall pop, a notice sheet dismissed in a second) has no picture. Taps are not events; the investigators' tap streams come from the replay export and were re-read by the adversary.
  • On Android the store sheet, the out-of-energy sheet, and the paywall are often not on camera: black frames or 25 to 400 second holes in 7 of 13 Android money sessions. Tap coordinates on some Android devices are scaled about 1.3x relative to the frame.
  • The warehouse had events loaded to about 2026-09-06 05:58 UTC when the synthesis ran and 08:57 UTC when the adversary re-checked, so any "never came back" means "not by then".
  • Two sampled paid-attributed Android devices in the new-user sample had only a 25 to 26 second recording each and were never watched, so that sample's denominator is 19 devices, not 21. Five of the 32 cold-read draws were web-source recordings with no frames and were skipped; 27 were watched.
  • Play vs AI events do not reach the warehouse for this window, so nothing about Play vs AI can be counted from the data.

Things we noticed about our own tracking

For the data owner. One line each. Nothing here is a headline.

  • No event marks a process death. session_summary.end_reason=process_start with explicit_close=false is on 1,414 of 1,522 summaries in the window (every session that is not a rollover), so it cannot serve as a restart marker; only the gap method (a cold start with no app_background before it) separates a death in the foreground from an ordinary background kill. Sentry does not report an operating-system kill.
  • paygate_checkout_finished.result_status is "cancelled" for all 18 rows in the window although 29 sessions were granted Plus; the grant is visible only in subscription_entitlement_transition (part of the closed purchase never recognized issue (psmobile #4922) (closed 2026-09-04)).
  • subscription_checkout_store_interaction writes a terminal row for 22 of 63 new-device store sheet opens; the other outcomes surface only in paygate_result and subscription_entitlement_transition. Any "no result" count must join all three.
  • After a store cancel on 2.1.41, the onboarding gate writes 8 to 10 paygate_open_attempted, post_auth_onboarding_blocked reason=unknown_route_removed, and paygate_result route_disposed rows inside one second before one presentation sticks (12 of 18 store-cancel sessions, 110 rows); the person sees one screen, and the rows inflate presentation counts.
  • The "username already taken" error has no event (profile_update_rejected is empty in the window) and identity_gate_completed.reason is always reserved_default_username, so the taken message cannot be counted.
  • push_prompt_metagate_shown is written twice for one showing (seen_count_before 2 and 3 within 100 ms) on iOS 2.1.41 build 130; and push_eligibility_snapshot before the day-2 prompt can read permission_status=not_determined with reason=permission_denied on the same row.
