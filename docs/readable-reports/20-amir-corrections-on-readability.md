# Amir's corrections on readability: a ledger from his own sessions

This file collects Amir's verbatim reactions to writing he found hard to read: jargon, bare codes, cognitive load, condescension, over-compression, tone, and report format. It exists so a readable-reports skill can be built on his real words instead of on guesses.

Companion sample: the report he quoted is saved at `/Users/aelaguiz/workspace/arch_skill/docs/readable-reports/samples/20260906-morning-priorities.txt`. The line he quoted reads "Checkout root now clean, children blocked." Its source markdown is `/Users/aelaguiz/workspace/psagentspace/roadmaps/company/morning-priorities/runs/2026-09-06.md`.

## How this was built

- Method: the installed `agent-history` skill (`/Users/aelaguiz/.agents/skills/agent-history/SKILL.md`) and its helper `scripts/agent_history.py prompts --scope all-projects --since 90d` for each runtime, then targeted reads of the matching session files to recover what the agent had written just before each correction.
- Window: 2026-06-08 to 2026-09-06. Runtimes: Prime Agent (2,049 prompt records, store begins 2026-08-11), Claude Code (26,445 prompt records from 2026-07-01), Codex (11,457 prompt records from `/Users/aelaguiz/.codex/history.jsonl`), Pi (50 records, all test prompts, nothing relevant).
- Search terms started with the list in the task (plain English, confusing, jargon, cognitive, wordy, idiot, child, spam me out, bottom line, and so on) and widened to his own phrasing as it appeared ("talk in English", "not formatted for readability", "written for robots not humans", "two ticks too complicated", "don't spam me out").
- Excluded on purpose: sessions in his personal legal projects (`logan`, `console`, `exhibits`, `personal`). Two quotes from the `snap` card-game side project are kept because they are about writing, not about the game.
- Evidence quality: every quote in this file is exact text from a stored user message or from the verdict corpus file named below. The "what the agent had written" column is a short excerpt of the assistant message immediately before the correction when the transcript is still on disk. Where the transcript was purged (Codex rollouts before 2026-06-27, several Claude project transcripts) the cell says so and the quote still comes from the global prompt store. Anything that is my reading rather than his words is marked as inference.
- Related source he already maintains: `/Users/aelaguiz/workspace/psagentspace/research/2026-07-18-amir-ai-expert-x-strategy/amir-draft-verdicts.md` (his verbatim verdicts on community drafts, with the draft beside each verdict). A few rows below cite it directly.

## Findings

Ten recurring shapes. Each has one representative quote, its source, and a one-paragraph reading. Finding numbers are reused in the ledger's last column.

1. **Bare codes, labels, and coined terms with no plain-English name.**
   Quote: "stop saying W3. I don't know what fucking W3 is. Talk in English."
   Source: Prime session 01a019dd-29ee-748b-910b-4d9c3f8ad6a6, 2026-08-19.
   Reading: W3, A3, F3, A7, L3, "three links", "reactivations", "until a real candidate certifies", and the report line "Checkout root now clean, children blocked" are the same failure. The writer used a token that only makes sense to someone holding the writer's context. Fix: name the thing in words the reader already uses, every time it appears.

2. **Paragraphs where a table, a few words, or one punchline belongs.**
   Quote: "It's too wordy and hard to read. You need to simplify your language and reduce the amount of words and get to simple punchlines quickly."
   Source: Prime session 01a04e16-3651-774f-8147-0da00428ddca, 2026-08-29.
   Reading: He says this about reports, slides, spreadsheet cells, and chat replies. His own numbers: notes of "five or seven words", one line of rationale, "simple tables". The complaint is density, not length alone.

3. **The answer is buried under receipts, verdicts, status codes, and history.**
   Quote: "Just tell me the fucking caveat. I don't want to read all this."
   Source: Prime session 01a05f13-fbdb-7334-88f7-4d89931a0dc3, 2026-09-04.
   Reading: Also: "What's, in plain English, what's between us and the plan being done?" and "You're telling me it's complete but not complete. It's nonsensical." Put the single answer first. Report one plain state (works, left, blocked on), not layers of done.

4. **The artifact leans on the conversation instead of standing alone.**
   Quote: "Please don't refer to earlier reconstructions. Nobody knows what you're talking about. I know for you it's obvious because you're thinking linearly right now but I've been working on this for 2 days. ... The slide just has to be contained."
   Source: Codex session 01a06e6c-3df9-77e2-a728-6630dd9b8af6, 2026-09-04.
   Reading: He built the systems and still says "pretend I'm not familiar with the fucking plan". Write for a reader with no session memory. He asked for cold reads by someone "in the seat of andrew or one of our investors".

5. **System vocabulary instead of what the user sees.**
   Quote: "you're not speaking in user facing terms it's hard to fucking understand what you're saying"
   Source: Codex session 019effbb-efb3-7c20-b321-fc4707c5548b, 2026-06-26.
   Reading: Flag names, component names, alert names, and status labels all drew the same reaction: "written for robots not humans". Say which screen, which tap, what appears, who is affected.

6. **Over-explaining and under-explaining at the same time.**
   Quote: "I don't understand, are you saying, even in simpler terms, our code doesn't do something or you don't know a fact that is external to our system. Like I can't tell. You're over explaining and under explaining simultaneously."
   Source: Codex session 019ebc52-427b-73f2-85c6-51bb7e8c18da, 2026-06-21.
   Reading: This is his "It's not just make things shorter" point. The text adds method, receipts, and architecture while the one load-bearing fact is missing. Progressive disclosure: simplest picture on top, detail underneath.

7. **Tone: talking down, or sounding mechanical and formulaic.**
   Quote: "I want you to read this for just plain English, like sixth-grade reading level. Don't talk to me like I'm an idiot but don't use jargon either."
   Source: Prime session 01a06982-a0ec-7559-b5b2-279c46273346, 2026-09-03.
   Reading: The bar has two sides. "one tick too dumbed down ... I dont' want to be spoken to like a child" and, in the same fortnight, "It's too wordy and hard to read". Plain words, real component names, adult register. Repeated shapes read as "robotic" and "sounds like AI".

8. **Framing: editorializing, dramatizing, or scolding the reader.**
   Quote: "You're framing it with this authority on the business that you actually haven't gone deep enough to understand our priorities and stuff."
   Source: Prime session 01a063fd-f708-7189-94c4-211297ae57fb, 2026-09-02.
   Reading: Also "Stop framing the website as a fucking problem" and, on a report paragraph that implied he ignores experiments, "remvoe this". Report what was observed and how often. Leave intent and priority to him. Pick verbs that say what happened ("omitted" hid a deploy regression).

9. **Shape he accepts: business outcome first, then tables; one unhedged sentence on top; numbered steps for plans.**
   Quote: "This is way too fucking wordy. It should just be simple tables and it should boil things down into what top-level initiative this fits under. We should think about tasks second and the business outcome first."
   Source: Codex session 01a076b5-910b-73a3-aab6-c1d59d599a12, 2026-09-06 (the morning priorities report).
   Reading: Related: "put a single top level commander's directive at the top. that should be one unmistakable sentence, not hedged", "show me the plan in numbered steps", "Give me a table, easy to read". Do not churn the structure between turns.

10. **A question to him must be one plain question with the choices named in words.**
   Quote: "What the fuck are you asking me in plain English?"
   Source: Codex session 01a06df4-2983-78b2-bc88-b4efaf39d4cd, 2026-09-04.
   Reading: Also "Ask me whatever question in plain English please", "What's the question?", "Stop asking me questions and fucking jargon ... Now what is question one?" The agent had wrapped the ask in issue numbers, skill doctrine, and status.

What he does not mean by "readable" (his words): "It's not just 'make things shorter,' right?" (Prime session 01a077bd-61f9-7316-a98e-ec5ef6a171c8, 2026-09-06) and "I don't want you to dumb down my data but I am talking aobut the FORMATTING AND BEST PRACTICES" (Prime session 01a072c8-fbb6-7769-a607-37e301d309e9, 2026-09-05). Inference: the target is low reconstruction cost for a smart reader, not fewer words or simpler ideas.
